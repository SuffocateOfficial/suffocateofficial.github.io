Today we're launching the **Suffocate app** – one home for everything Watch Me Suffocate.

Over the past months, Watch Me Suffocate has grown into more than music: the [SUFFOCATE club nights](https://club.watchmesuffocate.com), [Watch Them Slaughter](https://slaughter.watchmesuffocate.com) with Schlachthauß, the community token [Suffocate (WMS)](https://token.watchmesuffocate.com), [merch](https://merch.watchmesuffocate.com) and the [Watch Me](https://wm.geyser.at) projects. The app brings all of it together in one place.

## Open it now

The web app is live at **[app.watchmesuffocate.com](https://app.watchmesuffocate.com)**.

## What you can do

* **Sign in with your wallet.** Connect MetaMask and sign a message – no password, no account form.
* **See your token.** Your WMS balance, the total supply and the contract on Polygon at a glance – and add WMS to MetaMask with one tap.
* **Read the blog.** News and stories from Watch Me Suffocate. Some posts are unlocked by holding WMS.
* **Unlock goodies.** Perks for the community that you can unlock and claim in the app.
* **Donate.** Support the project by sending WMS directly from your wallet. How donations are used will be reported transparently on the blog.
* **Explore the ecosystem.** Club, merch, official, token and Watch Me – all in one place.

## Android

A native Android app, also called **Suffocate**, with the same features is on its way to Google Play. We'll announce it here as soon as it's available.

## What stays the same

The token, its contract and [token.watchmesuffocate.com](https://token.watchmesuffocate.com) keep running as before, and existing redeem codes still work. The club, merch and Watch Me sites stay where they are – the app simply connects them.

This site stays the official documentation of Watch Me Suffocate.

Thanks for being part of this.
