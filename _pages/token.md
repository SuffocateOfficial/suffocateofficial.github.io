**Suffocate (WMS)** is the community token of Watch Me Suffocate. It connects the band, the club nights and the people around them – holding WMS unlocks content and perks.

* Website: [token.watchmesuffocate.com](https://token.watchmesuffocate.com)
* App: [app.watchmesuffocate.com](https://app.watchmesuffocate.com)
* Instagram: [@watchmesuffocate.token](https://instagram.com/watchmesuffocate.token)

Facts
------
* Name / ticker: **Suffocate (WMS)**
* Network: **Polygon** (ERC-20)
* Initial supply: **1,000,000 WMS** ([initial distribution](/paper/#initial-distribution))
* Contract: [`0xe4C5b8aA4C575625648C13Ea036eCdFbE21f6961`](https://polygonscan.com/token/0xe4C5b8aA4C575625648C13Ea036eCdFbE21f6961)
* DAO wallet (donations): [`0xFE33e6D06E5f986553fcD8d60B416fA8bb299018`](https://polygonscan.com/address/0xFE33e6D06E5f986553fcD8d60B416fA8bb299018)
* Built on OpenZeppelin contracts: burnable, pausable, permit (gasless approvals), upgradeable

What WMS does
------
* **Tiers** – holding or donating WMS reaches a tier: Breath, Pulse, Suffocate, Void ([thresholds](/paper/#tiers))
* **Access** – members-only blog posts and exclusive content in the app, unlocked by tier
* **Goodies** – perks for members, unlocked and claimed in the app
* **Redeem codes** – redeem codes for WMS on the token site and in the app
* **Donations** – support the project by sending WMS to the DAO wallet; how donations are used is reported on the blog
* **Direct** – self-produced and independently managed, no intermediaries between the band and its community

Get started
------
1. Install [MetaMask](https://metamask.io) and switch to the Polygon network.
2. Open the [Suffocate app](https://app.watchmesuffocate.com) and sign in with your wallet – no password, no account form.
3. Add WMS to MetaMask with one tap and see your balance, the supply and the contract at a glance.
