**Suffocate** – everything in one place: your WMS token, the club nights, members-only posts and goodies, donations. One wallet login.

* Web app: [app.watchmesuffocate.com](https://app.watchmesuffocate.com)
* Android app coming to Google Play
* Launch post: [Introducing the Suffocate app](/posts/suffocate-app/)

Features
------
* **Wallet sign-in** – MetaMask / Sign-In with Ethereum, no password, free
* **Token** – your WMS balance and your tier: Breath, Pulse, Suffocate, Void
* **Members-only posts and goodies** – unlocked by tier
* **Club nights** – SUFFOCATE events, including the archive
* **Donations** – donate WMS, every donation counts on chain
* **Redeem codes** – redeem codes for WMS
* **Links** – official, merch, token and club

Without signing in, only the main page is available – everything else is for signed-in members.
